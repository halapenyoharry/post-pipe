## 1. Creative work is free

You may use post-pipe for creative work (writing, art, music, film, games, research, a portfolio, a story told as a graph) whatever you earn elsewhere, as long as the work you make with post-pipe is not itself the thing you sell. A free-to-read novel site counts as creative work even if its author sells the book in a shop.

## 2. Small commercial use is free

You may use post-pipe commercially while the revenue you get directly from works or services that use post-pipe stays under US$10,000 a year (any twelve months in a row). "Directly" means money paid for the thing post-pipe is part of: a paid site or subscription built on it, a commissioned piece made with it, a client project delivered with it. It does not mean your whole income.

## 3. Ask first for these

Open an issue titled "commercial licence" at <https://github.com/halapenyoharry/post-pipe/issues> before you:

- earn more than US$10,000 a year directly from works or services that use post-pipe;
- sell post-pipe itself, or a product built mainly from it;
- offer post-pipe, or something built mainly from it, to others as a hosted service.

I want creators to use this freely, and businesses to talk to me. A commercial licence is how that conversation ends well for both of us.
