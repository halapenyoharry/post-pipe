The branch library occupies a brick building on the corner, with tall sash windows that let in the afternoon light across four long oak tables. Even when every chair is taken, the room holds a steady, unhurried quiet.

Along the west wall, the reference shelves keep local maps, repair manuals, and bound volumes of old town directories within easy reach. A wooden cart by the desk holds returned books waiting to be wheeled back to their rows.

When rain begins to tap against the high glass panes, nobody hurries to leave. The brass lamps on the tables are switched on one by one, casting warm pools of light across open pages.
